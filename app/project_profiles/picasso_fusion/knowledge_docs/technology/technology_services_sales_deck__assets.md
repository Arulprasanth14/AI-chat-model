## Assets

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, product screenshots, charts, case studies, or visuals to include? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload available assets. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have photography, product screenshots, charts, case studies, or visuals.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
