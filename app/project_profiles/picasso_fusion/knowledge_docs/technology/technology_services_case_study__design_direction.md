## Design Direction

When conducting the Case Study brief for a Technology client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Any reference designs or inspiration? Share examples you like, competitor materials, or previous case studies. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload reference designs, up to 3. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes to reference designs above.

Ask the client: Describe the visual style you'd like. The available choices include: "Professional", "Corporate", "Premium", "Modern", "Minimal", "Bold", or other options.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
