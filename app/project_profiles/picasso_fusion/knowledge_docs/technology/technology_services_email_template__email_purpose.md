## Email Purpose

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Email Purpose** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What type of email template do you need? The available choices include: "Newsletter", "Product Updates", "Promotional Emails", "Event Invitations", "Customer Onboarding", "Customer Retention", or other options.
