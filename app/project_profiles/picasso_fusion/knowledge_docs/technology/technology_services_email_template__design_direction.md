## Design Direction

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Any reference email designs or inspiration? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload reference emails, up to 3. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes to reference email designs above.

Ask the client: Describe the email design style you'd like. The available choices include: "Minimal", "Corporate", "Premium", "Editorial", "Product-Focused", "Modern SaaS", or other options.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
