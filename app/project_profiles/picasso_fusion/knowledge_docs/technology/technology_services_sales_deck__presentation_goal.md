## Presentation Goal

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Presentation Goal** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this sales presentation? Examples: Generate leads, close a sale, secure a meeting, pitch a service, introduce a product, win a contract. The client may select one or more from: "Generate Leads", "Close a Sale", "Secure a Meeting", "Pitch a Service", "Introduce a Product", among others.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? Examples: Small business owners, enterprise buyers, HR managers, procurement teams, retailers, investors, healthcare professionals, etc. This is one of the most important questions because the design and messaging should be tailored to the audience. The client may select one or more from: "Small Business Owners", "Enterprise Buyers", "HR Managers", "Procurement Teams", "Retailers", among others.
