## Offer Details (Optional)

When conducting the Product One-Pager brief for a Technology client, the **Offer Details (Optional)** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Is there a specific offer, promotion, membership, or pricing information to include? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: What are the offer details? (this is not required) Offer, dates, what's included, pricing, and any conditions. Only if you said yes above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
