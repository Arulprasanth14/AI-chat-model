## Product / Service Information

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Product / Service Information** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What product, service, or solution are you presenting? Provide a brief description of the offering.

Ask the client: What are the key benefits or selling points? List the most important features, benefits, or competitive advantages to highlight.

Ask the client: Are there any offers, pricing details, memberships, or promotions to include? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: What are the offer details? (this is not required) Pricing, offer terms, dates, what's included, and any conditions. Only if you said yes above.

In this section, 3 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
