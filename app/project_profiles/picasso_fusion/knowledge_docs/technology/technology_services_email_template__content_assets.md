## Content & Assets

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Content & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, product images, illustrations, or brand assets to use? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload assets. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have photography, product images, illustrations, or brand assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
