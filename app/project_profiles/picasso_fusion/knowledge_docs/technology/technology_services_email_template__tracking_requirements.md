## Tracking Requirements

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Tracking Requirements** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Will links in this template require UTM tracking? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide UTM parameters or confirm AI-generated UTMs. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
