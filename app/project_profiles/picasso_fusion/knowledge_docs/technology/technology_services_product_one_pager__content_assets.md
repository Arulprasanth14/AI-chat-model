## Content & Assets

When conducting the Product One-Pager brief for a Technology client, the **Content & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What copy/text should appear on this one-pager? Please provide final content. Late copy changes may require additional revisions.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, product images, screenshots, or other visuals? Print assets should be a minimum of 300 DPI at final print size. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload print-ready images. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have photography, product images, screenshots, or other visuals.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
