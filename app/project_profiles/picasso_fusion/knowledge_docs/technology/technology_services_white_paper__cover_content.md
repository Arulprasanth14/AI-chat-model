## Cover Content

When conducting the White Paper brief for a Technology client, the **Cover Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What text should appear on the cover? Include title, subtitle, author name, company name, publication date, edition number, or any other required content.

Ask the client: Should the cover include any call to action? The available choices include: "Download the Full Report", "Visit Our Website", "Contact Us", "Scan a QR Code", "No Call to Action".

Optionally, ask the client: What is the destination URL for the QR code? (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected Scan a QR Code above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
