## Images & Assets

When conducting the White Paper brief for a Technology client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, illustrations, charts, or imagery for the cover? Print assets should be a minimum of 300 DPI at final print size. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload print-ready assets. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have photography, illustrations, charts, or imagery for the cover.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
