## Business Story

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Technology client, the **Business Story** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What problem does your company solve?

Ask the client: What is your solution?

Ask the client: What makes your solution different from competitors?

Ask the client: What are the key metrics, traction, or achievements to highlight? Examples: Revenue, Users, Growth rate, Customers, Retention, Partnerships, Market share. The client may select one or more from: "Revenue", "Users", "Growth Rate", "Customers", "Retention", among others.
