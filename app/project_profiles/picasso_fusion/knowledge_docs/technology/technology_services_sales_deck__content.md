## Content

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you already have presentation content prepared? The available choices include: "Yes, Complete Content Ready", "Partial Content Available", "Need Help Structuring Content", "Need Help Writing Content".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your presentation content, notes, or existing deck. (this is not required) Accepted formats: PPTX, PDF, DOCX, Google Slides export. Only if you have existing content.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
