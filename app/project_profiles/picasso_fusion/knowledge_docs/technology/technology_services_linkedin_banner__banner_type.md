## Banner Type

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Technology client, the **Banner Type** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Is this for a personal profile or a company page? The available choices include: "Personal LinkedIn Banner", "Company LinkedIn Banner".
