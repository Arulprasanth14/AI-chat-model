## Template Goal

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Template Goal** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this email template? Examples: Drive website traffic, Generate leads, Promote products, Share company updates, Register for events, Educate customers. The client may select one or more from: "Drive Website Traffic", "Generate Leads", "Promote Products", "Share Company Updates", "Register for Events", among others.
