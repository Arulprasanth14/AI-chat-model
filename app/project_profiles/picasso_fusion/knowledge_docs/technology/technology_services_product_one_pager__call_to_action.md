## Call to Action

When conducting the Product One-Pager brief for a Technology client, the **Call to Action** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What action should readers take after viewing this one-pager? The available choices include: "Visit Website", "Request a Demo", "Contact Sales", "Call Now", "Scan QR Code".

Optionally, ask the client: What is the destination URL for the QR code? (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected Scan QR Code above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
