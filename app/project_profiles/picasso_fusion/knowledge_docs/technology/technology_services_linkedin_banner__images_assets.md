## Images & Assets

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Technology client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, headshots, product visuals, or brand assets to use? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload images and assets. (this is not required) PNG or JPG format recommended. Only if you have photography, headshots, product visuals, or brand assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
