## One-Pager Overview

When conducting the Product One-Pager brief for a Technology client, the **One-Pager Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What product, service, or offering is this one-pager promoting? Provide the product/service name and a brief description.

Ask the client: What are the key benefits or selling points? List the most important features, benefits, or differentiators to highlight.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? Examples: Consumers, retailers, distributors, investors, enterprise buyers, healthcare professionals, etc. The client may select one or more from: "Consumers", "Retailers", "Distributors", "Investors", "Enterprise Buyers", among others.
