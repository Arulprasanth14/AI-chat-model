## Call to Action

When conducting the Case Study brief for a Technology client, the **Call to Action** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What action should readers take after viewing this case study? The available choices include: "Contact Us", "Book a Consultation", "Visit Our Website", "Scan a QR Code".

Optionally, ask the client: What is the destination URL for the QR code? (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected Scan a QR Code above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
