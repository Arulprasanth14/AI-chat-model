## Company Overview

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Technology client, the **Company Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is your company, product, or startup? Provide a brief overview of the business and what it does.

Ask the client: What stage is the business currently at? The available choices include: "Idea Stage", "MVP", "Early Revenue", "Growth Stage", "Established Business".
