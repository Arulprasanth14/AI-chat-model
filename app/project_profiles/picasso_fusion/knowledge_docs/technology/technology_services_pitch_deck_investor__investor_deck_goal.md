## Investor Deck Goal

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Technology client, the **Investor Deck Goal** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the primary purpose of this deck? The available choices include: "Raise Investment", "Secure Partnerships", "Sales Presentation", "Board Presentation", "Accelerator Application", "Internal Strategy Presentation".

Ask the client: Who is the intended audience? The client may select one or more from: "Angel Investors", "Venture Capital Firms", "Private Equity", "Strategic Partners", "Corporate Buyers", among others.
