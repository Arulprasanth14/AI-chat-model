## Story Overview

When conducting the Case Study brief for a Technology client, the **Story Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What project, client, or success story is this case study about? Provide a brief overview of the project.

Ask the client: What challenge or problem was being addressed? What was the situation before your solution was implemented?.

Ask the client: What solution was provided? Describe the service, product, or approach that solved the problem.

Ask the client: What results or outcomes should be highlighted? Include metrics, achievements, testimonials, or measurable impact.
