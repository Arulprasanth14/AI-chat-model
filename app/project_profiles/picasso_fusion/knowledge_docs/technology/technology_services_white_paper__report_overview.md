## Report Overview

When conducting the White Paper brief for a Technology client, the **Report Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the title of the white paper or report? Provide the exact title as it should appear on the cover.

Ask the client: What is the report about? Provide a brief summary of the report's topic, findings, or purpose.

Ask the client: Who is the intended audience? Examples: Executives, investors, healthcare professionals, policymakers, researchers, customers, etc.

Ask the client: How many pages does the report contain? This helps determine spine requirements and cover layout considerations.
