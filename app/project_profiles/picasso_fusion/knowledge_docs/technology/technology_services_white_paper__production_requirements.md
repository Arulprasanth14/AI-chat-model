## Production Requirements

When conducting the White Paper brief for a Technology client, the **Production Requirements** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Is this cover for print, digital PDF distribution, or both? This is actually more important than asking technical requirements first because digital report covers are designed differently from printed covers. The available choices include: "Print", "Digital PDF Distribution", "Both".

Optionally, ask the client: Any technical or print-specific requirements? (this is not required) Leave blank if unsure — we'll use standard specifications.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
