## Design Direction

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Any presentation designs or inspiration you'd like us to reference? Competitors, industry leaders, previous presentations, or styles you admire. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload reference presentations, up to 3. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes to reference designs above.

Ask the client: Describe the presentation style you'd like. Examples: Corporate, premium, modern SaaS, bold, minimal, enterprise, data-driven, luxury. The available choices include: "Corporate", "Premium", "Modern SaaS", "Bold", "Minimal", "Enterprise", or other options.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
