## Email Platform

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Email Platform** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Which email platform will this template be used in? This should be one of the first questions because it affects how the template is built. The available choices include: "Mailchimp", "Klaviyo", "HubSpot", "ActiveCampaign", "Brevo", "Campaign Monitor", or other options.
