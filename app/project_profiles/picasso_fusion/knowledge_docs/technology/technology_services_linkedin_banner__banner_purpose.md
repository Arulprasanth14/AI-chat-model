## Banner Purpose

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Technology client, the **Banner Purpose** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this LinkedIn banner? Examples: Personal branding, thought leadership, lead generation, recruiting, company awareness, product promotion, event promotion. The client may select one or more from: "Personal Branding", "Thought Leadership", "Lead Generation", "Recruiting", "Company Awareness", among others.
