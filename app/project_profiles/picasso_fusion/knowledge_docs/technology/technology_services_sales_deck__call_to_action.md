## Call to Action

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Technology client, the **Call to Action** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What action should the audience take after viewing the presentation? Examples: Book a demo, Request a proposal, Contact sales, Start a trial, Schedule a meeting. The available choices include: "Book a Demo", "Request a Proposal", "Contact Sales", "Start a Trial", "Schedule a Meeting".

Ask the client: Will the presentation include a QR code? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: What is the destination URL for the QR code? (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
