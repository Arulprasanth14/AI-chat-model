## Content Structure

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Technology client, the **Content Structure** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Which sections should be included in the template? The client may select one or more from: "Hero Banner", "Intro Text", "Featured Article", "Product Grid", "Event Section", among others.
