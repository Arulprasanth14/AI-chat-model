## Typical Project Lifecycle

1. **Discovery & Brief** — Creative brief intake (this conversation)
2. **Strategy** — Audience research, competitive analysis, creative territories
3. **Concepting** — 2–3 creative directions presented as mood boards + rationale
4. **Production** — Approved direction executed across all deliverables
5. **Review Rounds** — Typically 2–3 revision rounds included in scope
6. **Handoff** — Final files, brand guidelines, usage instructions delivered

Standard production timelines:
- Brand identity: 6–10 weeks
- Social media campaign: 3–5 weeks
- Website redesign: 8–14 weeks
- Content strategy + one month's assets: 2–4 weeks

## Pricing Guidance

Picasso AI operates on project-based and retainer models.

**Project-based:**
- Starter package ($5,000–$15,000): Single focused deliverable.
  Examples: logo + 1-pager brand guide, 4-week social content pack,
  1 landing page design.
- Growth package ($15,000–$50,000): Multi-deliverable campaign or brand system.
  Examples: full brand identity suite, 3-month content strategy + production,
  integrated launch campaign (social + paid + email).
- Enterprise ($50,000+): Transformational brand work or long-term partnership.
  Examples: rebrand across product portfolio, always-on creative retainer.

**Retainer model:**
Monthly retainers from $8,000/month covering a defined creative output quota.
Retainer clients receive priority scheduling and reduced per-asset rates.
