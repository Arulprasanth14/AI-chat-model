## Structure & Content

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Structure & Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the carousel be structured? Choose the storytelling style. The available choices include: "Product Features", "Product Comparison", "Collection Showcase", "Problem → Solution", "Before → After", "How to Use", or other options.

Ask the client: Do you already have the content? The available choices include: "Final Copy Ready", "Rough Points", "Need Copywriting Support".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your copy or rough points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have final copy ready or rough points.

Ask the client: Tell us the flow of the carousel. Describe what you'd like each slide to communicate. Example: Cover → New Summer Collection; Slide 2 → Product 1; Slide 3 → Product 2; Slide 4 → Key Benefits; Slide 5 → Customer Review; Slide 6 → Offer; Slide 7 → Shop Now.

Ask the client: What should the cover slide say? This is the first thing customers will see. Example: "Meet Our New Collection", "5 Products You'll Love", "Upgrade Your Everyday Essentials".

Ask the client: How many slides would you like? The available choices include: "5", "6", "7", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

In this section, 5 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
