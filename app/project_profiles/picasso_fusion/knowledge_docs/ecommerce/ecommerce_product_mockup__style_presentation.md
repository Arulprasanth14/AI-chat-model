## Style & Presentation

When conducting the Product Mockup brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Style & Presentation** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What style best suits your product? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Luxury", "Modern", "Minimal", "Lifestyle", among others.

Ask the client: What type of presentation would you like? The available choices include: "Studio Product Shot", "Lifestyle Scene", "Flat Lay", "Floating Product", "Product in Use", "Packaging Focus", or other options.
