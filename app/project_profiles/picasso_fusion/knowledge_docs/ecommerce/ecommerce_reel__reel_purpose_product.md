## Reel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Reel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Reel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this Reel. The available choices include: "Product Launch", "Product Showcase", "New Arrival", "Best Seller", "Product Demonstration", "Unboxing", or other options.

Ask the client: Which product are we featuring? Provide the key product details: Product Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits, Available Colours/Sizes/Variants (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should buy this product. Example: "Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours.", "Premium leather built to last.", "Designed for everyday comfort.".
