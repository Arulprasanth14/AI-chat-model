## Tone & Music

When conducting the Video brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Tone & Music** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the video feel? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Luxury", "Modern", "Lifestyle", "Minimal", among others.

Ask the client: What kind of music would you prefer? The available choices include: "Trending Social Audio", "Upbeat", "Electronic", "Cinematic", "Luxury", "Ambient", or other options.
