## Footage & Key Shots

When conducting the Video brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Footage & Key Shots** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What footage do you have? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Videos", "I'll Upload Lifestyle Videos", "I Have Both Videos & Photos", "Share via Google Drive", "Use Stock Footage", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload videos, photos, logos, packaging, or supporting assets. Recommended: MP4/MOV, 1080p or higher.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any product shots that must appear? (this is not required) Example: Packaging, Product close-up, Feature demonstration, Product in use, Unboxing, Colour variants, Accessories included.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
