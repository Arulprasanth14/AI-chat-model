## Output & Language

When conducting the Product Mockup brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Output & Language** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What output do you need? The client may select one or more from: "Web Optimized", "High Resolution", "Print Ready", "Transparent Background", "Multiple Versions", among others.

Ask the client: What language should appear on the mockup? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
