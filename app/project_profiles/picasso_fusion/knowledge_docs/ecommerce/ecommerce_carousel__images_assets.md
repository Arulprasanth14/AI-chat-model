## Images & Assets

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have product photos? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Photos", "I'll Upload Lifestyle Photos", "I'll Upload Both", "Use Stock Lifestyle Images", "Use Only Brand Assets", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload product photos, packaging, logos, or supporting assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
