## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this print piece? The client may select one or more from: "Increase In-Store Sales", "Promote a Product", "Launch a New Product", "Highlight an Offer", "Build Brand Awareness", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this for? Describe your target audience.
