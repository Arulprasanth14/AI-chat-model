## Images & Assets

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have product photos or artwork? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Photos", "I'll Upload Lifestyle Images", "I'll Upload Both", "Use Stock Images", "Use Only Brand Assets", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload product images, logos, packaging, or supporting artwork. For the best print quality, high-resolution images (300 DPI or higher) are recommended.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
