## Carousel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Carousel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this carousel. The available choices include: "Product Launch", "Product Showcase", "Collection Showcase", "New Arrivals", "Best Sellers", "Product Features", or other options.

Ask the client: Which product(s) are we featuring? Provide the key product information: Product Name(s), Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits, Colours/Sizes/Variants (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should buy these products. Example: "Designed for all-day comfort.", "Premium quality at an everyday price.", "The perfect gift for every occasion.".
