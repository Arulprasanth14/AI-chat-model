## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this digital asset? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Sales", "Promote a Product", "Launch a Collection", "Drive Website Traffic", "Generate Leads", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this for? Describe your ideal customer.
