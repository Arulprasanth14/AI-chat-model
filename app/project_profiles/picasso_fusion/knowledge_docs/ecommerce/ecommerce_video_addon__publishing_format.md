## Publishing & Format

When conducting the Video brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Publishing & Format** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Where will this video be published? Select all that apply. The client may select one or more from: "Product Page", "Amazon / Marketplace", "Instagram", "Facebook", "TikTok", among others.

Ask the client: What output formats do you need? The client may select one or more from: "Vertical (9:16)", "Square (1:1)", "Landscape (16:9)", "Multiple Formats", "Let the Creative Team Recommend".

Ask the client: What language should the video use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
