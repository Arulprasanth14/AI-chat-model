## Images & Assets

When conducting the Product Mockup brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have product images or artwork? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Photos", "I'll Upload Packaging Artwork", "I'll Upload Labels", "I'll Upload 3D Renders", "I Have Multiple Angles", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload product photos, packaging artwork, labels, logos, or transparent PNGs. For the best results, provide multiple angles or high-resolution images.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
