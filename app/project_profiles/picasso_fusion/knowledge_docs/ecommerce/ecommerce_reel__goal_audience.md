## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Reel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this Reel? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Sales", "Launch a Product", "Promote an Offer", "Drive Website Traffic", "Build Brand Awareness", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this product for? Describe your ideal customer.
