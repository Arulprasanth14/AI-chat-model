## Footage & Key Shots

When conducting the Reel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Footage & Key Shots** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What footage do you have? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Videos", "I'll Upload Lifestyle Videos", "I Have Both Videos & Photos", "Share via Google Drive", "Use Stock Footage", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload product videos, photos, logos, packaging, or supporting assets. Preferred format: Vertical (9:16), MP4/MOV, 1080p or higher.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any shots that must be included? (this is not required) Example: Product close-up, Packaging, Unboxing, Product in use, Feature demonstration, Customer reaction, Colour options.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
