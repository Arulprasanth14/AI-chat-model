## Publishing & Language

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Publishing & Language** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What should the final slide encourage customers to do? The available choices include: "Shop Now", "View Collection", "Buy Today", "Visit Website", "Send Us a DM", "Learn More", or other options.

Ask the client: Where will this carousel be published? The client may select one or more from: "Instagram (1:1)", "Instagram (4:5)", "Facebook", "LinkedIn", "Pinterest", among others.

Ask the client: What language should the carousel use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
