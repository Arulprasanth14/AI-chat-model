## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Ad Banner brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this campaign? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Sales", "Website Traffic", "Lead Generation", "Product Launch", "Promote an Offer", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this campaign for? Describe your target audience.
