## Video Purpose & Product

When conducting the Video brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Video Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this video. The available choices include: "Product Launch", "Product Showcase", "Product Demonstration", "How to Use", "Unboxing", "Customer Testimonial", or other options.

Ask the client: Which product(s) are we featuring? Provide the key product details: Product Name(s), Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits, Colours/Sizes/Variants (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should buy this product. Example: "Premium quality that lasts.", "Designed for everyday comfort.", "Fast charging with all-day battery life.".
