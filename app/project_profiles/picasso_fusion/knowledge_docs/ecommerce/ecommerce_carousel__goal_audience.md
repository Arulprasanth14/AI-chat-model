## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this carousel? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Sales", "Promote a Collection", "Launch a Product", "Highlight Features", "Educate Customers", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this carousel for? Describe your ideal customer.
