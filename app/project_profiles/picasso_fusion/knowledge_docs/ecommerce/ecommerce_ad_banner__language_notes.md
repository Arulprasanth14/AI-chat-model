## Language & Notes

When conducting the Ad Banner brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Language & Notes** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What language should the banner use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.

Optionally, ask the client: Is there anything you'd like us to avoid? (this is not required) Mention any colours, imagery, wording, competitor references, or visual directions you'd prefer not to use.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
