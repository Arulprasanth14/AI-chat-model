## Goal & Placement

When conducting the Product Mockup brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Goal & Placement** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this mockup? The available choices include: "Product Launch", "Website Listing", "Online Store", "Social Media", "Advertisement", "Packaging Preview", or other options.

Ask the client: Where will this mockup be used? The client may select one or more from: "Website", "Shopify Store", "Amazon / Marketplace", "Instagram", "Facebook", among others.
