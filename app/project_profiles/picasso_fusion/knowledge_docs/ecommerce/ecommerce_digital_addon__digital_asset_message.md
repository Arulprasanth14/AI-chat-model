## Digital Asset & Message

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Ecommerce client, the **Digital Asset & Message** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the digital asset. The available choices include: "Website Banner", "Homepage Hero Banner", "Email Header", "Email Newsletter (EDM)", "Google Display Ads", "Meta Ad Creative", or other options.

Ask the client: Which product(s) are we promoting? Provide the key product information: Product Name(s), Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits, Colours/Sizes/Variants (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should buy. Example: "Premium comfort for everyday wear.", "Limited-time festive collection.", "Fast delivery with free shipping.".
