## Contact & Social

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Contact & Social** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What contact information should appear? Only include details recipients should use. The client may select one or more from: "Full Name", "Job Title", "Department", "Phone Number", "Mobile Number", among others.

Ask the client: Should social media links be included? Social links can improve credibility and engagement. The client may select one or more from: "LinkedIn", "X / Twitter", "Instagram", "Facebook", "YouTube", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Provide social profile URLs. (this is not required) Please ensure all URLs are correct. Only if you selected social media links above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
