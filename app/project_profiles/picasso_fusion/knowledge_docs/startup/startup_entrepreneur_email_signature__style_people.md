## Style & People

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Style & People** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What style would you prefer? Choose a style that matches your brand. The available choices include: "Corporate", "Minimal", "Modern", "Premium", "Startup", "Creative", or other options.

Ask the client: Will employee headshots be included? The available choices include: "Yes", "No", "Optional".
