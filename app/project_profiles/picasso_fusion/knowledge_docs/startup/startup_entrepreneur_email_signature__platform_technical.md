## Platform & Technical

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Platform & Technical** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Which email platform(s) will use this signature? Rendering differs between platforms. The client may select one or more from: "Outlook", "Gmail", "Microsoft 365", "Apple Mail", "Thunderbird", among others.

Ask the client: Do you need HTML email signature files or design-only output? This is probably the most important technical question and is currently missing. The available choices include: "HTML Signature", "Design Mockup Only", "Both", "Not Sure".
