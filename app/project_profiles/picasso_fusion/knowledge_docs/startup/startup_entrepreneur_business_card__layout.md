## Layout

When conducting the Business Card brief for a Startup client, the **Layout** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Single-sided or double-sided card? Double-sided cards provide additional space for branding and QR codes. The available choices include: "Single-Sided", "Double-Sided".

Optionally, ask the client: Should any additional information appear? (this is not required) Only include information that adds value. The client may select one or more from: "Tagline", "Certifications", "Awards", "Service Categories", "Social Media Handles", among others.

Ask the client: What card orientation do you prefer? This affects layout significantly and is often forgotten. The available choices include: "Horizontal", "Vertical", "Square", "No Preference".

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
