## Quotes & Contact

When conducting the Press Kit brief for a Startup client, the **Quotes & Contact** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Are there approved quotes that media can use? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide approved spokesperson quotes. (this is not required) Examples: Founder quote, CEO statement, Investor quote, Customer quote. Only if you said yes above.

Ask the client: What contact information should media use? Ensure journalists can quickly reach the correct person. The client may select one or more from: "PR Contact", "Founder", "Marketing Team", "Agency Representative".

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
