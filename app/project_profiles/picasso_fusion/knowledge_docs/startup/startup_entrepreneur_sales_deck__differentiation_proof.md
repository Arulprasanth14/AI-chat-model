## Differentiation & Proof

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Startup client, the **Differentiation & Proof** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are your key differentiators? Why should someone choose you instead of competitors or alternatives?. The client may select one or more from: "Faster Implementation", "Lower Cost", "Better Results", "Proprietary Technology", "Industry Expertise".

Ask the client: What proof, results, or case studies can we include? Evidence increases trust and conversion. The client may select one or more from: "Client Success Stories", "Testimonials", "Revenue Growth", "Cost Savings", "Customer Metrics", among others.
