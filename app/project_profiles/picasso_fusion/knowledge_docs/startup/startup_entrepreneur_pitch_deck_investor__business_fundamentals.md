## Business Fundamentals

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Startup client, the **Business Fundamentals** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What problem are you solving? Investors invest in meaningful problems.

Ask the client: What is your solution? Clearly explain how your product or service solves the problem.

Ask the client: What is your business model? How does the company make money? The client may select one or more from: "SaaS Subscription", "Marketplace", "Transaction Fees", "Licensing", "Services", among others.
