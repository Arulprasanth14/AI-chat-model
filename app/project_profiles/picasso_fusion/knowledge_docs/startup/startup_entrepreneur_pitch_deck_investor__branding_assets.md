## Branding & Assets

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Startup client, the **Branding & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have branding and assets? Consistent branding improves investor perception. The available choices include: "Yes", "No", "Partial".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logos, screenshots, product images, and brand assets. (this is not required) PNG, SVG, JPG preferred. Only if you have branding assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
