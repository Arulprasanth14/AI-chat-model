## QR Code

When conducting the Business Card brief for a Startup client, the **QR Code** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Should a QR code be included? QR codes can improve engagement and lead generation. The available choices include: "Website", "Digital Business Card (vCard)", "LinkedIn Profile", "Booking Page", "Portfolio", "No QR Code".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide QR code destination URL. (this is not required) Please verify the link before production. Only if you selected a QR code option above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
