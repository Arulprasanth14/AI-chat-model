## Legal & Compliance

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Legal & Compliance** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Should a legal disclaimer or compliance notice be included? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide disclaimer text. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
