## Press Kit Overview

When conducting the Press Kit brief for a Startup client, the **Press Kit Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is this press kit for? Press kits should communicate one clear story. The available choices include: "Startup Launch", "Product Launch", "Funding Announcement", "Company Milestone", "Partnership Announcement", "Event Announcement", or other options.

Ask the client: What company, product, or announcement are we featuring? Provide a brief summary of the news or story.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience for this press kit? Different audiences require different messaging. The client may select one or more from: "Journalists", "Industry Publications", "Investors", "Partners", "Influencers", among others.
