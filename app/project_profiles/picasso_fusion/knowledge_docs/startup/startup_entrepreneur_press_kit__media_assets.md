## Media Assets

When conducting the Press Kit brief for a Startup client, the **Media Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography or imagery? High-quality media assets are essential. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logos, founder photos, executive headshots, team photos, screenshots, and product images. (this is not required) High-resolution files preferred. Only if you have photography or imagery.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
