## Offer & Call to Action

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Startup client, the **Offer & Call to Action** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What pricing or package information should be included? The available choices include: "Include Pricing", "Include Starting Price", "Contact for Pricing", "Not Required".

Ask the client: What is the primary call to action? The presentation should end with one clear next step. The available choices include: "Book a Demo", "Schedule a Call", "Request a Proposal", "Start Free Trial", "Contact Sales".
