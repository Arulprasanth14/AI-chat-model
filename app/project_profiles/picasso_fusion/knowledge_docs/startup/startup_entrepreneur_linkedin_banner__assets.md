## Assets

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Startup client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have imagery or brand assets? Quality visuals improve profile performance. The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logos, photos, headshots, screenshots, or brand assets. (this is not required) High-resolution files preferred. Only if you have imagery or brand assets.

Ask the client: Should any achievements, awards, certifications, or social proof be featured? Examples: Forbes Featured, 500+ Clients, Google Partner, Industry Awards. The client may select one or more from: "Forbes Featured", "500+ Clients", "Google Partner", "Industry Awards", "None".

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
