## Company & People

When conducting the Press Kit brief for a Startup client, the **Company & People** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What company information should appear? Provide an approved company description or boilerplate.

Ask the client: Should founder or executive profiles be included? The available choices include: "Founder Only", "Leadership Team", "No Profiles Required".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide founder/executive biographies and titles. (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected Founder Only or Leadership Team above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
