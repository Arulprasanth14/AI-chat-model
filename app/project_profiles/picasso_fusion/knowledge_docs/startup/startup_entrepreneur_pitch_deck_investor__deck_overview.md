## Deck Overview

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Startup client, the **Deck Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What stage of fundraising is this deck for? Investor expectations vary by stage. The available choices include: "Pre-Seed", "Seed", "Series A", "Series B+", "Angel Investment", "Accelerator / Incubator".

Ask the client: What company, product, or startup are we presenting? Brief overview of what the business does and who it serves.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience for this deck? Different investors expect different information. The client may select one or more from: "Angel Investors", "Venture Capital Firms", "Accelerators", "Strategic Partners", "Family Offices", among others.
