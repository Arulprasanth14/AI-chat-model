## Content

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Startup client, the **Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What key information should appear? Less is more. Include only essentials. The client may select one or more from: "Name", "Job Title", "Company Name", "Tagline", "Website", among others.
