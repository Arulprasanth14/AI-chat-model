## Card Overview

When conducting the Business Card brief for a Startup client, the **Card Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Who is this business card for? Provide the person's name and job title exactly as it should appear. Examples: "John Smith – Founder & CEO", "Sarah Lee – Senior Property Consultant", "Michael Chen – Marketing Director".

Ask the client: What is the primary purpose of this business card? Different goals require different layouts and information hierarchy. The available choices include: "Networking", "Sales & Lead Generation", "Client Meetings", "Corporate Identity", "Event Distribution", "Personal Branding".

Ask the client: What contact details should appear? Include only information recipients should use. The client may select one or more from: "Mobile Number", "Office Number", "Email Address", "Website", "Office Address", among others.
