## Traction & Funding Ask

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Startup client, the **Traction & Funding Ask** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What traction or proof do you have? Metrics build credibility. The client may select one or more from: "Revenue", "Users", "Growth Rate", "Customers", "Partnerships", among others.

Ask the client: How much funding are you seeking, and by when do you need it?

Ask the client: What will the funds primarily be used for? The client may select one or more from: "Product Development", "Hiring", "Marketing", "Expansion", "Operations".
