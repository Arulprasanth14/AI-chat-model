## Branding & Assets

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Branding & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have brand assets? Brand consistency improves trust and recognition. The available choices include: "Logo Only", "Full Brand Guidelines", "Existing Signature", "No Assets".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logo and brand assets. (this is not required) SVG or PNG preferred. Only if you have brand assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
