## Market & Competition

When conducting the Pitch Deck — Investor brief for a Startup client, the **Market & Competition** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What market opportunity should be highlighted? Market size, industry opportunity, growth trends, TAM/SAM/SOM, etc.

Ask the client: Who are your key competitors and what makes you different? Differentiation is one of the most important investor considerations.
