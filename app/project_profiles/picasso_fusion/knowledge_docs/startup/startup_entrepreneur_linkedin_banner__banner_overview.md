## Banner Overview

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Startup client, the **Banner Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Is this banner for a personal profile or company page? Banner content and messaging differ significantly. The available choices include: "Personal Profile", "Company Page".

Ask the client: What is the objective of this LinkedIn banner? One banner = one clear message. The client may select one or more from: "Personal Branding", "Thought Leadership", "Lead Generation", "Company Awareness", "Recruiting", among others.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? Tailor the message to the audience. The client may select one or more from: "Founders", "Business Owners", "Investors", "Recruiters", "Job Seekers", among others.
