## Problem & Solution

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Startup client, the **Problem & Solution** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What challenges or pain points does the audience face? Strong sales decks start with the customer's problem.

Ask the client: How does your solution solve those challenges? Focus on outcomes and value, not just features.
