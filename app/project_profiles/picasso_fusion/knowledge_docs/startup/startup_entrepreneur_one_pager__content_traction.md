## Content & Traction

When conducting the One-Pager — Company Overview brief for a Startup client, the **Content & Traction** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What key information should appear on the one-pager? Select the most important sections. Multi-select. The client may select one or more from: "Company Overview", "Product Overview", "Services", "Market Opportunity", "Business Model", among others.

Ask the client: What traction, results, or proof points should be highlighted? Credibility is often the most valuable section on a startup one-pager. The client may select one or more from: "Revenue", "Customers", "User Growth", "Funding Raised", "Partnerships", among others.
