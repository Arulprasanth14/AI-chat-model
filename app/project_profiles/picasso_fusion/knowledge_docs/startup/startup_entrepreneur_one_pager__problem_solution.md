## Problem & Solution

When conducting the One-Pager — Company Overview brief for a Startup client, the **Problem & Solution** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What problem does the company solve? Keep it concise and outcome-focused.

Ask the client: What is the solution or offering? Describe the product, service, or platform being presented.

Ask the client: What makes the company different? Highlight your competitive advantage or unique value proposition. The client may select one or more from: "Proprietary Technology", "Faster Delivery", "Lower Costs", "Industry Expertise", "Better Customer Experience".
