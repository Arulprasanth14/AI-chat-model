## Assets & Copy

When conducting the One-Pager — Company Overview brief for a Startup client, the **Assets & Copy** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have company assets and imagery? Visuals improve engagement and credibility. The available choices include: "Yes", "No", "Partial".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logos, screenshots, product visuals, team photos, or marketing assets. (this is not required) PNG, SVG, JPG preferred. Only if you have assets available.

Ask the client: What copy or text should appear? Provide approved content, key messages, or existing company descriptions.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
