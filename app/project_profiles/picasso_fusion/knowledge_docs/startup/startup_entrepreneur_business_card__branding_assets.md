## Branding & Assets

When conducting the Business Card brief for a Startup client, the **Branding & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have brand assets? Consistent branding creates a professional impression. The available choices include: "Full Brand Guidelines", "Logo Only", "Existing Business Card", "No Assets Available".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload logo, brand guide, and visual assets. (this is not required) Vector files (SVG, AI, EPS, PDF) preferred. Only if you have brand assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
