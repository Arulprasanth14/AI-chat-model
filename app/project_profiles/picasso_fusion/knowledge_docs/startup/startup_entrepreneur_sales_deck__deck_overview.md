## Deck Overview

When conducting the Sales Deck — Presentation brief for a Startup client, the **Deck Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is this sales presentation about? One deck = one conversion goal.

Ask the client: What product, service, or solution are we selling? Provide a brief overview including key benefits and differentiators.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? The deck should speak directly to the decision-maker. The client may select one or more from: "Founders", "SMB Owners", "Enterprise Buyers", "Marketing Teams", "Real Estate Developers", among others.
