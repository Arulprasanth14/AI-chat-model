## One-Pager Overview

When conducting the One-Pager — Company Overview brief for a Startup client, the **One-Pager Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the purpose of this one-pager? One-pager = one clear business story. The available choices include: "Investor Introduction", "Partnership Outreach", "Sales Leave-Behind", "Accelerator Application", "Media / PR", "General Company Overview".

Ask the client: What company, product, or startup are we presenting? Briefly describe what the business does.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? Content and messaging should match the reader. The client may select one or more from: "Investors", "Venture Capital Firms", "Partners", "Customers", "Enterprise Buyers", among others.
