## Call to Action

When conducting the Email Signature brief for a Startup client, the **Call to Action** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you want a call-to-action banner or promotional section? Email signatures can drive leads, bookings, or awareness. The available choices include: "Book a Call", "Visit Website", "Download Resource", "Event Promotion", "Product Promotion", "No CTA".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide CTA text and destination URL. (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected a CTA option above.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
