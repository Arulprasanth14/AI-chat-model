## Content & Highlights

When conducting the Press Kit brief for a Startup client, the **Content & Highlights** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What content should be included? Select all required sections. Multi-select. The client may select one or more from: "Company Overview", "Founder Biography", "Leadership Team", "Product Overview", "Funding Information", among others.

Ask the client: What key facts, statistics, or milestones should be highlighted? Journalists often use these directly in articles. The client may select one or more from: "Funding Raised", "Customers", "Users", "Revenue Milestones", "Growth Metrics", among others.
