## Style & References

When conducting the Business Card brief for a Startup client, the **Style & References** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Preferred card style? Match the card to the brand personality. The available choices include: "Corporate", "Minimal", "Premium", "Luxury", "Modern", "Creative", or other options.

Ask the client: Any reference business cards or inspiration? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload reference cards, up to 3. (this is not required) Note: Only if you said yes to reference business cards above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
