## Images & Assets

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Fitness client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have images or assets? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Images", "I'll Upload Product Photos", "I'll Upload Graphics", "Use Stock Images", "Use Only Brand Assets", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload logos, images, product photos, presentations, or supporting assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
