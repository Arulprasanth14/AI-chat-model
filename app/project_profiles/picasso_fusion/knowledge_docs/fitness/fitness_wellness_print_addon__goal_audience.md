## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Fitness client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this print piece? The client may select one or more from: "Promote a Product", "Promote a Service", "Announce an Event", "Generate Leads", "Increase Sales", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this for? Describe your target audience.

Ask the client: What action should people take? The available choices include: "Visit Website", "Call Us", "Visit Store", "Scan QR Code", "Book Appointment", "Buy Now", or other options.
