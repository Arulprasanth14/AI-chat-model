## Structure & Content

When conducting the Carousel Set brief for a Fitness client, the **Structure & Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the story be structured? The available choices include: "Tips & Tricks", "Step-by-Step Guide", "Common Mistakes", "Before → After", "Transformation Story", "Myth vs Fact", or other options.

Ask the client: Do you already have the content? The available choices include: "Final Content Ready", "I Have Rough Points", "I Need Help Writing It".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your rough points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected "I Have Rough Points" above.

Ask the client: Tell us the flow of the carousel. Instead of describing every slide separately, walk through the narrative. Example: Cover → "5 Fat Loss Mistakes"; Slide 2 → Mistake 1; Slide 3 → Mistake 2; Slide 4 → Mistake 3; Slide 5 → Solution; Slide 6 → Summary; Slide 7 → CTA. This gives designers freedom while still understanding the narrative.

In this section, 3 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
