## Footage & Key Shots

When conducting the Reel brief for a Fitness client, the **Footage & Key Shots** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What footage do you have? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Video Clips", "I'll Upload Photos", "I Have Both Videos & Photos", "Share via Google Drive", "Use Stock Footage", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your footage. (this is not required) Upload videos, photos, logos, or supporting assets. Preferred format: Vertical (9:16), MP4/MOV, 1080p or higher. Only if you'll be uploading your own video clips or photos.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any important shots that must be included? (this is not required) Example: Client transformation reveal, Trainer speaking to camera, Gym equipment, Before & after, Product close-up, Workout demonstration.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
