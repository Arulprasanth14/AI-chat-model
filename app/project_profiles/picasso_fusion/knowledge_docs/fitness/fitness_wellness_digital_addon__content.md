## Content

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Fitness client, the **Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are you promoting? Provide the important information: Product/Service Name, Offer Details, Price, Key Benefits, Dates, Terms & Conditions.

Ask the client: Do you already have the content? The available choices include: "Final Copy Ready", "Rough Points", "Need Copywriting Support".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your final copy or rough points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have final copy ready or rough points.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
