## Slide Count & Key Slides

When conducting the Carousel Set brief for a Fitness client, the **Slide Count & Key Slides** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How many slides would you like? The available choices include: "5", "6", "7", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

Ask the client: What should the cover slide say? This is the most important slide. Example: "Stop Making These Gym Mistakes", "7 Foods That Build Muscle", "The Beginner Workout Guide".

Ask the client: What should the final slide encourage people to do? The available choices include: "Follow for More", "Save This Post", "Share with a Friend", "Book a Consultation", "Join Our Program", "Visit Our Gym", or other options.
