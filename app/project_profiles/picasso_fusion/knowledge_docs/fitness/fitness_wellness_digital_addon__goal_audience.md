## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Fitness client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this asset? The client may select one or more from: "Generate Leads", "Increase Sales", "Website Traffic", "Brand Awareness", "Product Launch", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this for? Describe your target audience.

Ask the client: What action should people take? The available choices include: "Visit Website", "Shop Now", "Register", "Book Now", "Download", "Learn More", or other options.
