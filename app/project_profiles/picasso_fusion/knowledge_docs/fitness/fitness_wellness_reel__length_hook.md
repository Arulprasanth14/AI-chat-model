## Length & Hook

When conducting the Reel brief for a Fitness client, the **Length & Hook** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How long should the Reel be? The available choices include: "Under 15 Seconds", "15–30 Seconds", "30–60 Seconds", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

Ask the client: What's the most important first 3 seconds? If someone watches only the beginning, what absolutely must they see or understand?.
