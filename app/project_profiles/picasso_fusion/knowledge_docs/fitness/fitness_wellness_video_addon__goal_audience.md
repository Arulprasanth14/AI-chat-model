## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Video brief for a Fitness client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this video? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Brand Awareness", "Generate Leads", "Sell a Product", "Promote a Service", "Book Appointments", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this video for? Describe your target audience.

Ask the client: Who or what will be featured? Select all that apply. The client may select one or more from: "Founder / Team", "Customer", "Product", "Service", "Office / Store", among others.
