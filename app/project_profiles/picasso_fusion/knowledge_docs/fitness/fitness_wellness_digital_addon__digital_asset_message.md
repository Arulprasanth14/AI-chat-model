## Digital Asset & Message

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Fitness client, the **Digital Asset & Message** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the digital asset. The available choices include: "Website Banner", "Email Header", "Email Newsletter (EDM)", "Google Display Ads", "Meta Ad Creative", "LinkedIn Banner", or other options.

Ask the client: What's the one message people should remember? Describe the main purpose of this asset. Example: "Summer Sale – Up to 40% Off", "Register for our Webinar", "Introducing our new product".
