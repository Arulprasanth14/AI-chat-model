## Tone & On-Screen Text

When conducting the Reel brief for a Fitness client, the **Tone & On-Screen Text** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the Reel feel? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Modern", "Energetic", "Motivational", "Cinematic", among others.

Ask the client: Should the Reel include text on screen? The available choices include: "Full Captions", "Key Highlights", "Animated Headlines", "Minimal Text", "No Text".

Ask the client: What kind of music would you like? The available choices include: "Upbeat Workout", "Trending Social Audio", "Cinematic", "Motivational", "Electronic", "Hip-Hop", or other options.
