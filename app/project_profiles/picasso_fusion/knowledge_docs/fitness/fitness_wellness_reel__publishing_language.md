## Publishing & Language

When conducting the Reel brief for a Fitness client, the **Publishing & Language** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Where will this Reel be published? The client may select one or more from: "Instagram Reels", "Facebook Reels", "TikTok", "YouTube Shorts", "Multiple Platforms".

Ask the client: What language should the Reel use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
