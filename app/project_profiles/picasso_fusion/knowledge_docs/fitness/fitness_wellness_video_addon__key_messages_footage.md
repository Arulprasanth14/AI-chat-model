## Key Messages & Footage

When conducting the Video brief for a Fitness client, the **Key Messages & Footage** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are the three most important points this video should communicate? Keep it simple — focus on the key messages you want every viewer to remember.

Ask the client: Do you already have footage? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Video Clips", "I'll Upload Photos", "I Have Both Photos & Videos", "Share via Google Drive", "Use Stock Footage", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload videos, photos, logos, graphics, or supporting assets. Recommended: MP4/MOV, 1080p or higher. Only if you'll be uploading your own footage.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any important scenes that must appear? (this is not required) Example: Product demonstration, Customer testimonial, Team introduction, Factory tour, Before & after, Event highlights.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
