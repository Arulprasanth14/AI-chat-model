## Tone & Music

When conducting the Video brief for a Fitness client, the **Tone & Music** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the video feel? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Cinematic", "Modern", "Luxury", "Corporate", among others.

Ask the client: What kind of music would you prefer? The available choices include: "Cinematic", "Upbeat", "Corporate", "Emotional", "Luxury", "Ambient", or other options.
