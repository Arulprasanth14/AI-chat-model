## Video Purpose & Message

When conducting the Video brief for a Fitness client, the **Video Purpose & Message** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of the video. The available choices include: "Brand Video", "Product Video", "Service Promotion", "Advertisement", "Company Introduction", "Event Highlights", or other options.

Ask the client: What's the one message viewers should remember? Describe the single biggest takeaway. Example: "We build premium homes with transparent pricing.", "Our new skincare range is now available.", "Join our annual fitness challenge.".
