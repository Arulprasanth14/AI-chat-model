## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Reel brief for a Fitness client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this Reel? The client may select one or more from: "Generate Leads", "Promote Memberships", "Sell a Program", "Book a Free Trial", "Build Trust", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this Reel for? Describe your ideal audience. Example: Beginners, women, athletes, busy professionals, weight-loss clients, seniors, etc.

Ask the client: What should viewers feel after watching? Choose up to 2. The client may select one or more from: "Motivated", "Inspired", "Confident", "Excited", "Curious", among others.
