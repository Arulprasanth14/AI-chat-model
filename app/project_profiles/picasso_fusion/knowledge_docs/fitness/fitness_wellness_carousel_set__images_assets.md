## Images & Assets

When conducting the Carousel Set brief for a Fitness client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photos to include? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Photos", "Use Stock Fitness Images", "Use Only Brand Assets", "Graphics & Icons Only", "Designer Can Decide".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your images. (this is not required) Label images if they belong to a specific slide. Only if you selected "I'll Upload Photos" above.

Ask the client: What brand assets should be used? The client may select one or more from: "Use My Brand Kit", "Upload Logo", "Upload Brand Guidelines", "Customize for This Project".

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your logo and brand guidelines. (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected Upload Logo or Upload Brand Guidelines above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
