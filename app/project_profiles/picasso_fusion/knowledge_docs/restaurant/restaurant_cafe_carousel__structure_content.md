## Structure & Content

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Structure & Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What type of carousel is this? The available choices include: "Menu Showcase", "Listicle (Top 5 / Top 10)", "Storytelling", "Step-by-Step Process", "Tips & Facts", "Before & After", or other options.

Ask the client: How many slides would you like? The available choices include: "5 Slides", "6 Slides", "7 Slides", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

Ask the client: What content should each slide include? Provide the information for each slide. Examples: Cover, Slide 2, Slide 3, Slide 4, Slide 5, Final CTA.
