## Content

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Restaurant client, the **Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you already have the final content? The available choices include: "Final Copy Ready", "Rough Points", "Need Copywriting Support".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your final copy or rough points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have final copy ready or rough points.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
