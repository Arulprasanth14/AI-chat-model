## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this carousel? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Restaurant Visits", "Increase Online Orders", "Promote a Menu", "Showcase Multiple Dishes", "Build Brand Awareness", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this carousel for? Describe your ideal customer.
