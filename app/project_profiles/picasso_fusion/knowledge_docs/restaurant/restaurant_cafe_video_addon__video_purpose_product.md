## Video Purpose & Product

When conducting the Video brief for a Restaurant client, the **Video Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the type of video. The available choices include: "Brand Film", "Featured Dish", "New Menu Launch", "Chef's Special", "Cooking Process", "Restaurant Tour", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we featuring? Provide the key details: Dish/Menu/Event Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Ingredients/Highlights.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest takeaway after watching the video. Example: "Fresh pasta made daily.", "Our summer menu is now available.", "Reserve your table for our live music night.".
