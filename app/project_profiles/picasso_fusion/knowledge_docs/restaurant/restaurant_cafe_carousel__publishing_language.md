## Publishing & Language

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Publishing & Language** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Where will this carousel be published? The client may select one or more from: "Instagram (1:1)", "Instagram (4:5)", "LinkedIn PDF Carousel", "Facebook", "Multiple Platforms", among others.

Ask the client: What should customers do after the final slide? The available choices include: "Visit Our Restaurant", "Order Online", "Reserve a Table", "Call Us", "Send Us a DM", "View the Full Menu", or other options.

Ask the client: What language should the carousel use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
