## Publishing & Language

When conducting the Static Post brief for a Restaurant client, the **Publishing & Language** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Where will this post be published? The client may select one or more from: "Instagram Feed (1:1)", "Instagram Portrait (4:5)", "Facebook", "LinkedIn", "X", among others.

Ask the client: What language should the copy use? The available choices include: "English", "Hindi", "Tamil", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", or other options.
