## Carousel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Carousel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this carousel. The available choices include: "Menu Showcase", "Featured Dishes", "New Menu Launch", "Combo Meal", "Chef's Recommendation", "Recipe / Behind the Kitchen", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we showcasing? Provide the key details: Dish/Menu/Campaign Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Ingredients/Highlights.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest takeaway after they've finished the carousel. Example: "Our new brunch menu is now available.", "Five must-try desserts this summer.", "Discover how we prepare our signature pizza.".
