## Images & Assets

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Restaurant client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography or artwork? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Food Photos", "I'll Upload Restaurant Ambience Photos", "I'll Upload Menu Images", "I'll Upload Illustrations or Graphics", "Use Stock Images Where Suitable", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload food photography, restaurant images, logos, menus, or supporting artwork. For the best print quality, high-resolution images (300 DPI or higher) are recommended.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
