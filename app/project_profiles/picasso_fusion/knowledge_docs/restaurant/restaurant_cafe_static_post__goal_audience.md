## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Static Post brief for a Restaurant client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this post? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Restaurant Visits", "Increase Online Orders", "Promote a Dish", "Launch a New Menu", "Promote an Offer", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this post for? Describe your ideal customer.
