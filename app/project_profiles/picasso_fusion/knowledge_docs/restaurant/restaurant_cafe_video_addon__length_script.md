## Length & Script

When conducting the Video brief for a Restaurant client, the **Length & Script** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Approximately how long should the final video be? The available choices include: "Under 30 Seconds", "30–60 Seconds", "1–3 Minutes", "3–5 Minutes", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

Ask the client: Do you already have a script? The available choices include: "Final Script Ready", "Rough Talking Points", "Need Scriptwriting Support", "No Narration Required".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your script or rough talking points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you selected "Final Script Ready" or "Rough Talking Points" above.

In this section, 2 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
