## Images & Assets

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photos or assets? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Food Photos", "I'll Upload Restaurant Ambience Photos", "I'll Upload Chef/Team Photos", "I'll Upload All Available Assets", "Use Stock Images Where Suitable", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload food photography, restaurant images, logos, menus, or supporting assets.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
