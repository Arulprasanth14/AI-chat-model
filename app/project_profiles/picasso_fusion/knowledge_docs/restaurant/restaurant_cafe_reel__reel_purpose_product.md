## Reel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Reel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Reel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this reel. The available choices include: "Featured Dish", "New Menu Launch", "Chef's Special", "Cooking Process", "Behind the Kitchen", "Restaurant Tour", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we featuring? Provide the key details: Dish/Menu/Event Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Ingredients/Highlights.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should visit or order. Example: "Fresh pasta made from scratch every day.", "Our biggest burger is finally here.", "Weekend brunch starts this Saturday.".
