## Footage & Key Shots

When conducting the Reel brief for a Restaurant client, the **Footage & Key Shots** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What footage do you have? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Food Preparation Videos", "I'll Upload Finished Dish Videos", "I'll Upload Restaurant Ambience", "I'll Upload Customer Experience Footage", "I Have Both Videos & Photos", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload videos, photos, logos, menus, or supporting assets. Recommended: MP4/MOV, Vertical (9:16) preferred, 1080p or higher.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any moments that must appear? (this is not required) Examples: Ingredients, Cooking process, Flames/Grilling, Cheese pull, Sauce pour, Plating, Steam, Chef preparing the dish, Customer reaction, Restaurant ambience, Offer announcement.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
