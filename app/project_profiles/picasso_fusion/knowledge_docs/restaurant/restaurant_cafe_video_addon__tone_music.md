## Tone & Music

When conducting the Video brief for a Restaurant client, the **Tone & Music** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the video feel? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Luxury", "Rustic", "Warm", "Cozy", among others.

Ask the client: What kind of music would you prefer? The available choices include: "Trending Instagram Audio", "Upbeat", "Acoustic", "Jazz", "Ambient", "Cinematic", or other options.
