## Digital Asset & Message

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Restaurant client, the **Digital Asset & Message** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the digital asset. The available choices include: "Website Banner", "Homepage Hero Banner", "Email Newsletter (EDM)", "Email Header", "Google Display Ads", "Meta Ad Creative", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we promoting? Provide the key details: Dish/Menu/Event Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Ingredients/Highlights.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest takeaway after seeing this digital asset. Example: "Try our new wood-fired pizzas.", "Weekend brunch is now available.", "Order directly and enjoy exclusive offers.".
