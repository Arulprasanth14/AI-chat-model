## Images & Assets

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Restaurant client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have images or assets? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Food Photos", "I'll Upload Restaurant Ambience Photos", "I'll Upload Menu Graphics", "I'll Upload Logos and Brand Assets", "Use Stock Images Where Suitable", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload food photography, restaurant images, menus, logos, or supporting assets. Recommended formats: PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
