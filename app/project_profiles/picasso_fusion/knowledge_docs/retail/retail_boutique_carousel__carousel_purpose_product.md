## Carousel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Retail client, the **Carousel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this carousel. The available choices include: "New Collection Launch", "Product Showcase", "Seasonal Collection", "Best Sellers", "New Arrivals", "Gift Guide", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we showcasing? Provide the key details: Product(s)/Collection Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should swipe through and visit your store or explore your collection. Example: "Discover our latest summer collection.", "Find the perfect gift for every occasion.", "See why customers love our handcrafted furniture.".
