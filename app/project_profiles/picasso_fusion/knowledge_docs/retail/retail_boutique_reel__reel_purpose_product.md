## Reel Purpose & Product

When conducting the Reel brief for a Retail client, the **Reel Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this reel. The available choices include: "New Collection Launch", "New Arrivals", "Product Showcase", "Store Tour", "Seasonal Campaign", "Sale / Offer", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we featuring? Provide the key details: Product/Collection/Event Name, Category, Key Features/Benefits, Price (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should watch and visit your store. Example: "Our summer collection has arrived.", "Visit us for exclusive in-store offers.", "See how we style this season's best looks.".
