## Tone & Music

When conducting the Reel brief for a Retail client, the **Tone & Music** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the reel feel? Choose up to 3. The client may select one or more from: "Premium", "Luxury", "Modern", "Minimal", "Lifestyle", among others.

Ask the client: What kind of music would you prefer? The available choices include: "Trending Instagram Audio", "Upbeat", "Pop", "Electronic", "Ambient", "Cinematic", or other options.
