## Footage & Key Shots

When conducting the Reel brief for a Retail client, the **Footage & Key Shots** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What footage do you have? The client may select one or more from: "I'll Upload Product Videos", "I'll Upload Store Walkthroughs", "I'll Upload Customer/Lifestyle Footage", "I Have Both Videos & Photos", "Share via Google Drive", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Upload your files. (this is not required) Upload videos, photos, logos, or supporting assets. Recommended: MP4/MOV, Vertical (9:16) preferred, 1080p or higher.

Optionally, ask the client: Are there any moments that must appear? (this is not required) Examples: Store entrance, Product close-ups, Staff interaction, Customer shopping, Collection reveal, Offer announcement, Checkout experience.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
