## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Retail client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this carousel? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Store Visits", "Promote a Collection", "Showcase Multiple Products", "Educate Customers", "Launch New Arrivals", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this carousel for? Describe your ideal customer.
