## Post Purpose & Product

When conducting the Static Post brief for a Retail client, the **Post Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the purpose of this post. The available choices include: "New Arrival", "Featured Product", "Collection Showcase", "In-Store Promotion", "Seasonal Sale", "Festive Campaign", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we promoting? Provide the key details: Product/Collection/Service Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should visit your store or choose your brand. Example: "New arrivals just landed.", "Premium fashion at festive prices.", "Visit us for our biggest sale of the year.".
