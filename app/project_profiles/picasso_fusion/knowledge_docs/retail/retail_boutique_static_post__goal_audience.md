## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Static Post brief for a Retail client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this post? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Store Visits", "Promote a Product", "Launch a Collection", "Promote an Offer", "Build Brand Awareness", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this post for? Describe your ideal customer.
