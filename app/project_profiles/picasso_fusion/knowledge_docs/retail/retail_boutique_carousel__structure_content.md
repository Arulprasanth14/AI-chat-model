## Structure & Content

When conducting the Carousel brief for a Retail client, the **Structure & Content** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: How should the carousel be structured? Choose the storytelling style. The available choices include: "Collection Showcase", "Product Highlights", "Buying Guide", "Before → After", "Tips & Inspiration", "Behind the Scenes", or other options.

Ask the client: Do you already have the content? The available choices include: "Final Copy Ready", "Rough Points", "Need Copywriting Support".

Optionally, ask the client: Provide your copy or rough points. (this is not required) Note: Only if you have final copy ready or rough points.

Ask the client: Tell us the flow of the carousel. Describe what you'd like each slide to communicate. Example: Cover → New Autumn Collection; Slide 2 → Featured Product 1; Slide 3 → Featured Product 2; Slide 4 → Key Benefits; Slide 5 → In-store Experience; Slide 6 → Offer; Slide 7 → Visit Our Store.

Ask the client: What should the cover slide say? This is the first thing customers will see. Example: "New Collection Has Arrived", "7 Must-Have Home Décor Pieces", "Why Everyone Loves Our Boutique".

Ask the client: How many slides would you like? The available choices include: "5", "6", "7", "Let the Creative Team Decide".

In this section, 5 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
