## Video Purpose & Product

When conducting the Video brief for a Retail client, the **Video Purpose & Product** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the type of video. The available choices include: "Brand Film", "Store Tour", "Collection Launch", "Product Showcase", "Seasonal Campaign", "Sale / Offer", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we featuring? Provide the key details: Product/Collection/Campaign Name, Category, Key Features/Benefits, Price (Optional).

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should visit your store or choose your brand. Example: "Experience our newest collection in-store.", "Luxury furniture designed for modern living.", "Visit us this weekend for exclusive offers.".
