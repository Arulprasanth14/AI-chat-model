## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Video brief for a Retail client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this video? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Store Visits", "Promote a Collection", "Showcase Products", "Build Brand Awareness", "Promote a Sale", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this video for? Describe your ideal customer.
