## Goal & Audience

When conducting the Print Design brief for a Retail client, the **Goal & Audience** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What's the goal of this print piece? The client may select one or more from: "Increase Store Visits", "Promote a Product", "Launch a Collection", "Promote a Sale", "Build Brand Awareness", among others.

Ask the client: Who is this for? Describe your ideal customer.
