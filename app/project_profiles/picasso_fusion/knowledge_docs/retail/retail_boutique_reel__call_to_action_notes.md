## Call to Action & Notes

When conducting the Reel brief for a Retail client, the **Call to Action & Notes** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What should viewers do after watching? The available choices include: "Visit Our Store", "Explore the Collection", "Shop Now", "Contact Us", "Send Us a DM", "Follow Our Page", or other options.

Optionally, ask the client: Is there anything you'd like us to avoid? (this is not required) Mention any editing styles, colours, music, wording, or visual directions you'd prefer not to use.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
