## Digital Asset & Message

When conducting the Digital Asset brief for a Retail client, the **Digital Asset & Message** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are we creating today? Select the digital asset. The available choices include: "Website Banner", "Homepage Hero Banner", "Email Newsletter (EDM)", "Email Header", "Google Display Ads", "Meta Ad Creative", or other options.

Ask the client: What are we promoting? Provide the key details: Product/Collection/Campaign Name, Category, Price (Optional), Key Features/Benefits.

Ask the client: What's the ONE message customers should remember? Describe the biggest reason someone should engage with this campaign. Example: "Visit our store for exclusive festive offers.", "Discover our newest furniture collection.", "Limited-time sale available this weekend only.".
