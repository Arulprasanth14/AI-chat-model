## Photography & Assets

When conducting the Buyer / Seller Guide brief for a Real Estate client, the **Photography & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, illustrations, or visuals for the guide? Please upload images and assets. Minimum 300 DPI for print.

Ask the client: Are there any additional assets to include? The client may select one or more from: "Agent photos", "Testimonials", "Case studies", "Checklists", "Infographics", among others.
