## Assets

When conducting the YouTube Banner brief for a Real Estate client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have headshots, logos, product images, or brand assets to include? Please upload the assets.

Ask the client: Will the creator's face be featured on thumbnails? A very important thumbnail-design question. The available choices include: "Yes", "No", "Sometimes".
