## Delivery Requirements

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Delivery Requirements** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What format is required? The available choices include: "PowerPoint (.pptx)", "Google Slides", "PDF", "Multiple Formats".

Ask the client: Will the CMA be presented live or sent as a standalone document? A critical question that's easy to miss — a live presentation can be visually lighter, while a standalone CMA needs more explanatory content. The available choices include: "Presented Live", "Sent as PDF", "Both".
