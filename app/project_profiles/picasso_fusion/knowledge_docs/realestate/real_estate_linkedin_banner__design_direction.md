## Design Direction

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Real Estate client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Optionally, ask the client: Any LinkedIn banners or profiles you'd like us to reference? (this is not required) Upload references, up to 3.

Ask the client: Describe the visual style you'd like. The available choices include: "Corporate", "Premium", "Minimal", "Modern", "Technology-Focused", "Luxury", or other options.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
