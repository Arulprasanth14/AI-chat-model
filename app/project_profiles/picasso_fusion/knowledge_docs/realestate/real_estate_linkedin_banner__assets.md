## Assets

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Real Estate client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have photography, headshots, product visuals, or brand assets to include? Please upload images and assets. PNG or JPG preferred.

Ask the client: Should any specific logos, certifications, awards, or achievements be featured? The client may select one or more from: "Industry Awards", "Partner Badges", "Certifications", "Company Logos", "None".
