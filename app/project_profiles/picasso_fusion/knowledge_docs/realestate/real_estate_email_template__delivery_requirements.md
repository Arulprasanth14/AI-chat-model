## Delivery Requirements

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Real Estate client, the **Delivery Requirements** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you need a fully coded HTML email or a design-only template? One of the most important questions here — easy to miss. The available choices include: "Fully coded HTML email", "Design-only template", "Both", "Not sure".

Ask the client: Will this template be used on desktop, mobile, or both? The available choices include: "Desktop", "Mobile", "Responsive (Both)".
