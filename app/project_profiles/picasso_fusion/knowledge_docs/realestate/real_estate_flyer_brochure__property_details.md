## Property Details

When conducting the Property Flyer / Brochure brief for a Real Estate client, the **Property Details** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What property is being promoted? Give the property name, address, and suburb/city.

Ask the client: What type of property is it? The available choices include: "House", "Apartment", "Villa", "Townhouse", "Commercial Property", "Land", or other options.

Ask the client: Is the property for sale, lease, or rent? The available choices include: "For Sale", "For Lease", "For Rent", "Auction", "Off-Market Listing".

Ask the client: What is the listing price? Price, price range, auction information, or "Contact Agent".

Ask the client: What key property details should be featured? Examples: bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, land size, building area, floor area, year built.

Ask the client: What are the property's key selling points? List the most important features buyers should notice immediately. The client may select one or more from: "Waterfront views", "Premium location", "Renovated interiors", "Near schools and transport", "Luxury finishes", among others.
