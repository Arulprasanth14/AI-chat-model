## Comparable Sales Data

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Comparable Sales Data** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What comparable sales should be included? Please provide all comparable properties and sale information.

Ask the client: Please upload comparable sales data. Accepted formats: Excel, PDF, CSV, property reports.

Ask the client: Should active listings or pending sales be included? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".
