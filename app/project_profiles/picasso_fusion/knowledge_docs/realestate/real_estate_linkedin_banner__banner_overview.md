## Banner Overview

When conducting the LinkedIn Banner brief for a Real Estate client, the **Banner Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Is this banner for a personal profile or a company page? The available choices include: "Personal LinkedIn Banner", "Company LinkedIn Banner".

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this banner? The client may select one or more from: "Personal Branding", "Thought Leadership", "Company Awareness", "Lead Generation", "Recruiting", among others.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? This is one of the most important questions. The client may select one or more from: "Business Owners", "Executives", "Recruiters", "Job Seekers", "Investors", among others.
