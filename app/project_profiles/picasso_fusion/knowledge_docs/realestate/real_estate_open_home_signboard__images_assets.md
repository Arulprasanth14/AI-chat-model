## Images & Assets

When conducting the Open Home Signboard brief for a Real Estate client, the **Images & Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have property photography to include? Please upload the images. Minimum 300 DPI at final print size.

Ask the client: Do you have any additional assets? The client may select one or more from: "Agent Photo", "Floor Plan", "Agency Awards", "QR Code", "Property Logo", among others.
