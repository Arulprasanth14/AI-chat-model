## Market Insights

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Market Insights** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What market data should be included? The client may select one or more from: "Median sale price", "Days on market", "Sales volume", "Auction clearance rates", "Market trends", among others.

Optionally, ask the client: Please upload any market data and reports. (this is not required)

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
