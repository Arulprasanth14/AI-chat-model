## Content & Data

When conducting the Real Estate Market Report brief for a Real Estate client, the **Content & Data** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What data and insights should be included? The client may select one or more from: "Median sale price", "Median rent", "Days on market", "Clearance rates", "Sales volume", among others.

Ask the client: Do you have the data prepared? The available choices include: "Yes, final data ready", "Partial data available", "Need help organising data".

Ask the client: Please upload your data files. Accepted formats: Excel, CSV, PDF, Google Sheets export.

Ask the client: What written commentary or analysis should appear in the report? Please provide final, approved copy.
