## Assets

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Real Estate client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have imagery, graphics, or brand assets to include? Please upload images and assets. PNG or JPG preferred. Web-optimised assets are acceptable.
