## Guide Overview

When conducting the Buyer / Seller Guide brief for a Real Estate client, the **Guide Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What type of guide is this? The available choices include: "First Home Buyer Guide", "Property Buyer Guide", "Property Seller Guide", "Investor Guide", "Downsizer Guide", "Suburb Guide", or other options.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? This is one of the most important questions — it affects the tone, content hierarchy, and visual style. The client may select one or more from: "First-time buyers", "Property investors", "Homeowners preparing to sell", "Downsizers", "Luxury property buyers", among others.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this guide? The client may select one or more from: "Generate appraisal leads", "Educate buyers", "Educate sellers", "Build trust and authority", "Generate consultation bookings", among others.
