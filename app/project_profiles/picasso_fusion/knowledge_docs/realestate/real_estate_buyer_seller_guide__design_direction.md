## Design Direction

When conducting the Buyer / Seller Guide brief for a Real Estate client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Optionally, ask the client: Any guide designs or publications you'd like us to reference? (this is not required) Examples: competitor buyer guides, seller guides, real estate magazines, previous editions. Upload reference designs, up to 3.

Ask the client: Describe the visual style you'd like. The available choices include: "Luxury Real Estate", "Premium Residential", "Educational", "Editorial", "Corporate", "Modern Minimal".

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
