## Assets

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Assets** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Do you have property photography to include? Please upload the images.

Ask the client: Do you have any additional assets? The client may select one or more from: "Maps", "Floor Plans", "Location Highlights", "Lifestyle Photography", "Agent Profile", among others.
