## Email Template Overview

When conducting the Email Template brief for a Real Estate client, the **Email Template Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What type of email template do you need? Select the template's primary purpose. The available choices include: "Newsletter", "Promotional Email", "Product Updates", "Event Invitations", "Customer Onboarding", "Customer Retention", or other options.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this email template? The client may select one or more from: "Drive website traffic", "Generate leads", "Promote products", "Share company updates", "Register for events", among others.
