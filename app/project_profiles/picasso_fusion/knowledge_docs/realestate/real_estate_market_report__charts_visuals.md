## Charts & Visuals

When conducting the Real Estate Market Report brief for a Real Estate client, the **Charts & Visuals** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Should any charts, graphs, or infographics be included? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: Do you have existing charts, or should they be designed from the supplied data? (this is not required) Note: Only ask this if the client said yes to including charts above. The available choices include: "I have existing charts to provide", "Design from the supplied data".

Optionally, ask the client: Do you have photography or imagery for the report? Please upload the images. (this is not required) Minimum 300 DPI for print.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 2 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
