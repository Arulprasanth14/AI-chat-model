## Report Overview

When conducting the Real Estate Market Report brief for a Real Estate client, the **Report Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What market or area does this report cover? The available choices include: "Suburb Market Report", "City Market Report", "Commercial Property Market", "Rental Market Report", "Luxury Property Market", "Investment Market Report".

Ask the client: What reporting period does this report cover? The available choices include: "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Half-Yearly", "Annual", "Custom Date Range".

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of this report? The client may select one or more from: "Generate seller leads", "Position the agency as a market expert", "Inform property owners", "Attract investors", "Support marketing campaigns".
