## Directional Requirements

When conducting the Open Home Signboard brief for a Real Estate client, the **Directional Requirements** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: Does the signboard need directional arrows? The available choices include: "Yes", "No".

Optionally, ask the client: What direction should be shown? (this is not required) One of the most commonly missed questions for open home signage — only ask this if the client said yes to directional arrows above. The client may select one or more from: "Left", "Right", "Straight Ahead", "Multiple Directions".

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
