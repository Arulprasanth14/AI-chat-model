## Property Details

When conducting the Real Estate Listing Sheet brief for a Real Estate client, the **Property Details** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What property is being promoted? Give the property name, address, and suburb/city.

Ask the client: What type of property is it? The available choices include: "Residential House", "Apartment", "Villa", "Townhouse", "Commercial Property", "Land", or other options.

Ask the client: Is this property for sale, lease, or rent? The available choices include: "For Sale", "For Lease", "For Rent", "Off-Market Listing".

Ask the client: What is the listing price? Asking price, price range, auction details, or "Contact Agent".

Ask the client: What are the key property details — bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, land size, building size, and year built?

Ask the client: What are the property's key selling points — the features buyers should notice first? Examples: ocean views, recently renovated, near schools, premium location, smart home features, investment opportunity. The client may select one or more from: "Ocean views", "Recently renovated", "Near schools", "Premium location", "Smart home features", among others.
