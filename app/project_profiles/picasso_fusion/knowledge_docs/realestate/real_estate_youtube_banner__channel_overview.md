## Channel Overview

When conducting the YouTube Banner brief for a Real Estate client, the **Channel Overview** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What is the YouTube channel about? The client may select one or more from: "Business & Entrepreneurship", "Real Estate", "Gaming", "Education", "Finance", among others.

Ask the client: Who is the target audience? The client may select one or more from: "First Home Buyers", "Business Owners", "Investors", "Students", "Developers", among others.

Ask the client: What is the primary goal of the channel? The client may select one or more from: "Grow Subscribers", "Build Personal Brand", "Generate Leads", "Educate Audience", "Sell Products", among others.
