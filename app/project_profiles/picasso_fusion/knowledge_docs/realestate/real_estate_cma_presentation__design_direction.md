## Design Direction

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Optionally, ask the client: Any CMA presentations or real estate reports you'd like us to reference? (this is not required) Upload reference presentations, up to 3.

Ask the client: Describe the desired presentation style. The available choices include: "Premium Real Estate", "Luxury", "Modern Corporate", "Data-Driven", "Editorial".

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
