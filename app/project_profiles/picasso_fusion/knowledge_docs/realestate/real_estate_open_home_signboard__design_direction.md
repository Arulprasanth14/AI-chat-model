## Design Direction

When conducting the Open Home Signboard brief for a Real Estate client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Optionally, ask the client: Any reference signboards or inspiration? (this is not required) Competitors, previous campaigns, or styles you admire. Upload references, up to 3.

Ask the client: Describe the visual style you'd like. The available choices include: "Premium Real Estate", "Luxury", "Minimal", "Corporate", "High Visibility", "Modern".

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
