## Property Information

When conducting the CMA Presentation brief for a Real Estate client, the **Property Information** section covers the following key information.

Ask the client: What are the key property details? Examples: bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, land size, building area, year built.

Ask the client: What are the property's key selling points? The client may select one or more from: "Renovated", "Waterfront", "School Zone", "Corner Block", "Development Potential", among others.
