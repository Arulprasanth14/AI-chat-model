## Design Direction

When conducting the Real Estate Listing Sheet brief for a Real Estate client, the **Design Direction** section covers the following key information.

Optionally, ask the client: Any reference designs or inspiration to share? Competitors, agency examples, previous listings, or styles you admire. (this is not required) Up to 3 references.

Ask the client: Describe the design style you'd like. The available choices include: "Luxury", "Modern", "Premium Residential", "Commercial Corporate", "Minimal", "High-End Real Estate", or other options.

In this section, 1 question(s) are required and 1 are optional — follow up on optional items only when contextually appropriate.
